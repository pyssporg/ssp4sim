# UC-016: Schedule Simulation Lifecycle Operations with Tight Timing

- **Actor:** user with a schedule of time-triggered lifecycle events.
- **Goal:** "schedule pause, speed, and stop at specific simulation times for a deterministic, pre-planned run."
- **Outcome:** each scheduled operation executes at the first macro-step boundary at or after its requested simulation time; the run completes without manual lifecycle control; results are preserved up to the last completed timestep.
- **Capability:** unified scheduling substrate for lifecycle and injection (`CP-001`).
- **Status:** implemented through `pause`, `stop`, and `set_speed` with an optional `at`; a command is deferred to the first macro-step boundary at or after that time, so activation granularity is one macro step. `resume` has no scheduled form.
- **Related:** `REQ-016`.
