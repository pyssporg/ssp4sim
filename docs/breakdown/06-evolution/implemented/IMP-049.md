# Remove the recorder from the executor

**Question:** Should the recorder be removed from the executor and instead injected into the models earlier?

**Evidence:** HEAD has no recorder field or use under `simulation/executor/`; the recorder lives in `SimulatorCore` and is registered with model storages during pre-setup (`sim_setup.cpp:19-23`).

**Change:** Move recorder injection out of the executor into model construction, and rewire all consumers that reach the recorder through the executor.

**Risk:** Unknown — the note is unmeasured; recorder consumers and the construction path must be audited before the move.

**Acceptance:** Resolved by `10e5b8c`: the executor no longer owns a recorder; `SimulatorCore` passes it to `pre::setup_sim_behaviour`, which registers model storages (`sim_setup.cpp:19-23`). Injection at model construction was not adopted; recording behavior and tests are unchanged.
